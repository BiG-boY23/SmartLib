Student's Library Guide

Official EVSU Library Policies & Regulations

Official policies, loan privileges, borrowing deadlines, overdue fine structures, and conduct rules from the EVSU Student's Library Guide.

Book Loan Privileges

  • Students: 3 books of different titles for overnight use
  • Faculty Members: 5 books of different titles for 7 days
  • Textbooks & books stamped "NOT FOR HOME USE" are room use only
  • Borrowing IDs are strictly non-transferable (no proxy borrowing)
Loan Privileges

Borrowing & Returning Hours

  • Borrowing Window: Monday–Friday, 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM
  • Returning Deadline: On or before due date at 9:00 AM
  • Open 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM continuously (No Noon Break)
  • All clients must sign the log-book at the depository area upon entry
Schedule & Deadlines

Library Fines & Lost Books

  • Overnight late returns: ₱1.50 per hour
  • Daily overdue fine: ₱10.00 per day
  • Lost book: Fine discontinued upon immediate report
  • Book replacement must be completed within two (2) weeks or fine reinstates
Fines & Penalties

Suspension of Privileges

  • 1st Offense: One (1) week suspension of privileges
  • 2nd Offense: One (1) month suspension of privileges
  • 3rd Offense: One (1) trimester / semester suspension
  • Grounds: Lending ID, unauthorized removal, vandalism, defacing pages, misconduct
3-Strike Disciplinary System

Use of the Library & Rules

  • Deposit bags at the baggage counter before entering reading areas
  • Handle all books and reading materials with care; do not deface
  • Do not reshelve books: Leave books on tables after using
  • Public Displays of Affection (PDA) are strictly prohibited
  • Always present validated School ID or COR (for freshmen)
House Rules

Sections & Community Access

  • 6 Sections: Reference, Periodical, Reserved, Circulation, New Books, E-Section
  • Access: Bonafide members of the EVSU academic community
  • External researchers: by formal request and Campus Director approval
  • Services: Circulation, Reference, Orientation, Referral, Current Awareness, Internet

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common library service questions.

How do I borrow a book from the library?
Present your valid EVSU School ID / Library ID at the Circulation counter. Borrowing of books for overnight use is scheduled from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Ensure you have no outstanding fines or overdue books before requesting a loan.
How many books can I borrow, and what is the loan duration?
Students (undergraduate and graduate) may borrow up to three (3) books of different titles strictly for overnight use. Faculty and academic staff may borrow up to five (5) books of different titles for seven (7) days. Books marked "NOT FOR HOME USE" and General Reference materials are strictly for room-use only.
When must borrowed books be returned?
Overnight books must be returned on or before 9:00 AM the following school morning. The library circulation counter is open continuously from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM with No Noon Break for early returns.
What are the fines for late or overdue books?
Overdue overnight books incur a fine of ₱1.50 per hour or ₱10.00 per day. Borrowers with overdue books or standing obligations to the library will not be allowed to borrow materials until all library accounts are settled.
What if I lose a borrowed book?
Report the loss immediately to the circulation librarian. The calculation of overdue fines will freeze as of the report date. You will be given a grace period of two (2) weeks to locate or replace the book with an acceptable copy. If not replaced within 2 weeks, overdue fines will resume until fully settled.
What are the disciplinary penalties for library infractions?
Suspension of library privileges is enforced for violations: 1st Offense results in a 1-week suspension; 2nd Offense results in a 1-month suspension; 3rd Offense results in suspension for the entire semester/trimester.
Who are the official library personnel if I need assistance?
The library is staffed by Romulo Joseph M. Jereza, IV, RL, MLIS (Supervising Librarian), Neña Divina D. Fevidal, RL, MLIS (Librarian Designate), and Library Assistants Ivan Jarvis C. Becamon, Jacqueline P. Duero, and Ronel P. Mas.
